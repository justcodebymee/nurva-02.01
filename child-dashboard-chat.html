🛡️ Stay Safe Online

Cyberbullying is repeated hurtful behavior through digital spaces, such as chats, games, or social media.

What can you do?
  • Do not reply with insults or threats.
  • Save relevant messages if it is safe to do so.
  • Block or report the account using the app's safety tools.
  • Tell a trusted adult, such as a parent, teacher, or school counselor.
It is not your fault. You do not have to handle it alone. Ask a trusted adult to help you.
